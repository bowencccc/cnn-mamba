# Published inference checkpoints

## Human four-state chr1-held-out model

`human_joint_frame_beta010_chr1held_epoch4_inference.pt` is the inference-only
form of the completed Cybertron Human four-state model. It retains the exact
epoch-4 `model_state_dict`, model configuration and validation provenance, but
omits optimizer, gradient-scaler and RNG state. It is suitable for scoring and
evaluation, but not for exactly resuming the interrupted optimizer trajectory.

- architecture: 10 kb CNN-Mamba-k7, 5 kb stride, frame dilation 3;
- four-state head: non-CDS/frame-0/frame-1/frame-2;
- joint auxiliary coefficient: 0.1; noncoding within-head weight: 0.1;
- training regime: chr1 held out; five epochs; best epoch 4;
- portable checkpoint SHA256:
  `429904082463e9822ec83aa15cfd48b16cf0cdb6ff15ff4e7141316117f9c037`;
- source full checkpoint SHA256:
  `30dc6f082d59acb9e8b84d2659e68b6fdaacf49bdeb1025c09364862e9acc287`.

The full 52 MiB resumable checkpoint remains external. Its optimizer state is
not needed for Rockfish inference tests. See the matching result report under
`results/human_joint_frame_beta010_v1/chr1held/`.

## Drosophila four-state chrX-in-training models

`drosophila_joint_frame_beta010_chrxtrain_epoch23_inference.pt` is the current
best Drosophila-evidence model used for UniAnn augmentation. It uses 10 kb
windows, 5 kb stride, CNN-Mamba-k7, dilation 3, a four-state auxiliary weight
of 0.1 and noncoding weight 0.1. ChrX was included in training by design for
the downstream annotation use case. Its chrX+ mean candidate AUPRC is 0.932868;
with joint-softmax T=9, EviAnn+UniAnn strict locus F1 is 90.399.

- best epoch: 23;
- portable checkpoint SHA256:
  `1ba47211b89350468740e63372262f1785bb5dcb4eb46c5862edaae34250dea7`;
- source full checkpoint SHA256:
  `97c13b17e80b14ef193224b2dcbebd4b04ed2feb13c42b4b9ada27b907c9419a`.

`drosophila_coleoptera_evidence_joint_frame_beta010_chrxtrain_epoch21_inference.pt`
uses the identical architecture and optimizer recipe, but its Dmel training
pseudo-labels were generated by EviAnn from Coleoptera protein evidence.

- best epoch: 21; early stopping occurred after epoch 24;
- best validation mean AUPRC: 0.897269;
- chrX+ mean candidate AUPRC: 0.907035;
- Coleoptera-EviAnn+UniAnn strict locus F1: 83.585;
- portable checkpoint SHA256:
  `8a936967e2ea1cffd64512d3e818c14864f4fa6904d841a90ad479ebf78179ef`;
- source full checkpoint SHA256:
  `a6e13a662ba560595cd7a247e1053a94e499e8ac3ee8f14dcf0ab94f7d0640a9`.

Both files are inference-only checkpoints. Full optimizer/scaler/RNG state is
kept outside Git.
